Custom dress tailoring in Choeng Thale

The Outfit Custom Tailor is a bespoke tailoring shop on Bang Tao Beach in Choeng Thale, Phuket. Kubir Dungana founded it in 2010. Everything is cut on the premises, and most pieces are ready in 24 to 48 hours over two to three fittings. The shop travels to hotels and villas in Bangtao Beach, Cherngtalay, Laguna, Surin and the wider Thalang district at no extra charge.

The shop makes women's bespoke tailoring: dresses, suits, blazers and occasion wear.
